# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

Batch Compress Video is developed by [3thousand30](https://3thousand30.com).

---

## What we collect

3thousand30 does not collect, store, or receive your videos, filenames, compression settings, or output files.

- No analytics sent to us
- No telemetry sent to us
- No crash reporting sent to us
- No account or registration required

---

## What leaves your device

Video processing happens locally through the FFmpeg engine bundled with the app. BCV does not upload video files or compression settings to a server.

When you choose a support, documentation, Store, or review link, the app opens that selected link in your default application. The destination service’s own privacy policy applies to that visit.

---

## Data stored on your device

BCV stores only ordinary local interface preferences, such as whether you dismissed the first-run Getting Started guide. Input videos remain in their original folder, and output videos are written to the output folder you choose.

---

## FFmpeg

BCV includes FFmpeg for local video processing. The applicable FFmpeg licence information is included with the installed app.
